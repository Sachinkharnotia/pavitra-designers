Featured Spotlight

Kanchipuram Silk

Timeless Craftsmanship

Draped in history and luxury, our Kanchipuram collection represents the pinnacle of pure mulberry silk weaving. Every thread is hand-loomed with gold zari thread in three distinct sections (korvai), creating the heavy pallus and borders that have marked celebrations for generations.
